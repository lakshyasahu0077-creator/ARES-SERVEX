Planetary Telemetry & Biometric Privacy Policy

DOCUMENT REF: NASA-CCSDS-PRIVACY-SEC-9 | CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED ASTRONAUTICAL DATA | STATUS: ACTIVE

1. Overview of Planetary Telemetry Collection

The ARES-SURVEX platform records, computes, and transmits real-time environmental, cartographic, and astronautical data to support surface survival during Martian extravehicular operations. This policy defines the classification, encryption, and dissemination of crew biometrics, life-support metrics, and planetary sample discoveries.

2. Astronaut Biometric & Physiological Confidentiality

During extravehicular traverses, the EMU-M4 suit stream monitors physiological indicators including metabolic oxygen consumption rate, heart rate variability, skin temperature, hydration status, and ionizing radiation dosimeter accumulators.

  • Medical Privacy: Raw biometric telemetry is encrypted under AES-GCM-256 adhering to Consultative Committee for Space Data Systems (CCSDS) standard 355.0-B-1 and is accessible exclusively by designated Flight Surgeons.
  • Aggregated Operational Telemetry: ARES-SURVEX extracts only de-identified consumable percentages (O2 margin, battery Wh, and RTB vector) for general tactical mission display on the public operations console.

3. Scientific Discovery & Planetary Sample Embargo

In accordance with the International Mars Sample Return Science Protocol:

  • Geochemical spectra, isotope ratios, and potential biosignature indicators collected via the Science Sample Registry remain under strict 12-month embargo to allow verification by the joint Principal Investigator team.
  • Basic spatial coordinates and lithology classifications (e.g. mudstone, olivine cumulate) are published immediately to ensure crew coordination and avoid redundant drilling operations.

4. Relay Encryption & Deep Space Network (DSN) Transmissions

Surface-to-orbit transmissions relayed via MRO, Odyssey, or TGO orbiters are protected against electronic interference and malicious spoofing using hardware-based cryptographic security modules (CSMs). Position coordinate logs are timestamped using high-stability rubidium atomic clocks synchronized to Mars Coordinated Time (MTC).

5. Planetary Cartographic Open Access

High-resolution topographic models (MOLA/HRSC datasets), slope gradient hazard classifications, and dust opacity (tau) measurements generated by ARES-SURVEX belong to the global scientific commons and are made available without proprietary restriction for the safety of all future interplanetary missions.
